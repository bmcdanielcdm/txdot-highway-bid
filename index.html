TxDOT Highway Bid Program

Unit-price worksheet for a Texas letting. Not an official bid form. Bids are submitted in iCX.

Download the workbook, paste a Whitley & Siddons EasyLinkPro export on the WS Import sheet, and price the job.

Download the workbook Source on GitHub

How a job gets in

  1. In EasyLinkPro, export the bid-item list to Excel.
  2. Open the WS Import sheet.
  3. Paste CSJ, highway, county, district, letting date, and the engineer's estimate into the yellow header.
  4. Paste items under row 15: bid code, description, unit, quantity.

The proposal, Item 500 mobilization, 2% bid guaranty, and category summary follow that sheet.
